# Aurion Trips Privacy Policy

_Effective: October 12, 2026 · Applies to the Aurion Trips iOS app_

Aurion Trips ("the app") is published by **AURION TECHNOLOGY LLC**, a Washington limited liability company ("Aurion", "we", "us").

## The short version
- Your trip video is made **entirely on your iPhone**.
- **Your photos are never uploaded** to us or anyone else.
- There is **no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking**.
- We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
- Your first video is free. The app may offer an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock more videos.

## What the app accesses on your device
- **Photos you choose.** With your permission, the app reads the photos you pick, plus each photo's stored location and capture time. It uses them to place your stops on a map, order them, and render your video. This happens on your device.
- **Saving to Photos.** If you tap Save, the app adds the finished video to your photo library (add-only permission).
- **Videos you create** are stored in the app's private storage on your device until you delete them or remove the app.

## What leaves your device
- **Map tiles.** To draw the map, the app downloads map tiles from **OpenFreeMap** (tiles.openfreemap.org) for the area your trip covers. Like any website, that server sees your IP address and which map tiles are requested. Those tiles show the general area of your trip. No photos, names or device identifiers are sent. See https://openfreemap.org for its practices.
- **Place names.** The app uses Apple's geocoding service (part of iOS) to turn stop coordinates into place names and local time zones. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **Sharing.** If you share a video, it goes only to the app or person you choose in the iOS share sheet.

We do not operate servers that receive your data from the app.

## In-app purchases
If the app offers an optional in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Your purchase record is kept by Apple and checked on your device to unlock features. You can restore it in the app.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices
You can change photo access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Aurion Trips. Deleting the app removes the videos and cached map data it stored.

## Your rights (including California and Washington residents)
We do not collect or sell personal information, so we don't hold your data to access, correct or delete. If you have questions, contact us and we will respond.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
