# Aurion Trips Support

Turn your trip photos into a vertical map video, made on your iPhone.

**Contact:** support@hiaurion.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

## Getting started
1. Open Aurion Trips and tap **Choose Photos**. Allow photo access.
2. Pick the photos from your trip. Photos that have a location are placed on the map automatically.
3. Check your route, edit the video title if you like, and tap **Make Video**.
4. Keep Aurion open while it renders (usually under a minute). Then **Save to Photos** or **Share**.

## FAQ
**Some photos don't appear on the map.**
They probably have no location data, for example screenshots, downloaded images, or photos taken with Location Services off for the Camera. You can place a missing photo by hand on the pin screen.

**I chose "Limit Access" and photos are missing.**
iOS only lets Aurion see the photos you selected. Tap **Add More** in the app, or go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Aurion Trips → Full Access.

**The map is blank or the video won't finish.**
Map tiles need an internet connection. Check your connection and try again. Keep the app open in the foreground until the video finishes.

**Are my photos uploaded?**
No. Everything is made on your iPhone. Only map tiles are downloaded. See our [Privacy Policy](https://hiaurion.com/trips/privacy).

**Is it free?**
Your first video is free, with no watermark. An optional one-time in-app purchase may be offered to unlock more videos. Purchases are handled by Apple. To restore a purchase on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

**Which devices are supported?**
iPhone with iOS 15 or later.

**Where does the map come from?**
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, © OpenMapTiles, tiles hosted by OpenFreeMap.
